# Phone Repair POS — Distribution & Listing Research

**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Author:** manager agent
**Status:** Research only — no product changes.

## Context

Phone Repair POS (PRPOS) is a global, free-forever-core point-of-sale iOS app supporting both iPhone and iPad, aimed at independent phone repair shops worldwide. A Pro tier is planned. Current state:

- £0 lifetime revenue.
- ~64 homepage impressions / 28 days in Google Search Console.
- SEO content shipping but slow to compound.

This document ranks non-SEO acquisition surfaces (directories, communities, launchpads, listicles) so future sessions can execute listing work without re-researching. No cold outreach is included — only places that accept submissions, listings, or community participation. Audience numbers are best-effort; any unverifiable claim is marked **unverified**.

## 4. Launchpads

PRPOS is an iOS app, so launchpads are most useful for: building a backlink graph, recruiting a few power users, and seeding reviews.

| # | Platform | Link | Audience | Effort | Impact | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Product Hunt** | producthunt.com | Largest launch audience (high) | Med (need hunter + assets) | High | Position as "free POS for repair shops, native iOS" |
| 2 | **Hacker News Show HN** | news.ycombinator.com | Tech-heavy, indirect (med) | Low (one post) | Low/Med | Frame as engineering/indie story, not as repair-shop pitch |
| 3 | **Indie Hackers** | indiehackers.com | Indie builders (high relevance for support, low for direct customers) | Low | Low (direct) / High (network) | Post product + revenue update |
| 4 | **BetaList** | betalist.com | Pre-launch waitlist audience; 4–6 wk free queue or paid (verified in search) | Low | Low | Past prime if already live, but good backlink |
| 5 | **Microlaunch** | microlaunch.net | Small indie crowd | Low | Low | Cheap shot |
| 6 | **Tiny Launch** | tinylaun.ch | Free, weekly ranking | Low | Low | Cheap shot |
| 7 | **Peerlist Launchpad** | peerlist.io | Professional/portfolio crowd | Low | Low | Backlink + dev audience |
| 8 | **Uneed / Smol Launch / Startupbase / Evalyze** | various | Aggregator launchpads | Low (bundled) | Low | Submit in batch in one sitting |
| 9 | **Launching Next** | launchingnext.com | Small | Low | Low | Backlink |

Sequence to use: BetaList (if still pre-Pro tier) → Tiny Launch / Microlaunch / Peerlist (warm-up week) → Product Hunt (main event) → Show HN follow-up two weeks later.
